# THE WORLD — Tuesday 24 November 2026

A classic broadsheet front page summing up Tuesday 24 November 2026's world
news in one image. The masthead carries the dateline, a UTC timeline strip
runs across the page, and four lead stories — the Hormuz mines and strait
closure, the ICJ's genocide ruling against Myanmar, the killing of Hamas's
internal security chief in Gaza despite the ceasefire, and the Villavicencio
car bomb — sit above a dated UTC story index covering 20 more stories across
the Middle East, Europe, Asia, the Americas, Africa, Oceania and
space/science, from Haftar's visit to Turkey to the COP31 collapse and the
Nvidia reinstatement.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`, failing loudly if the image is missing, over 1 MiB, or
neither PNG nor JPEG.
